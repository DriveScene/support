Privacy policy

DriveScene 3D - NSW · Effective 30 September 2026

This policy applies to the DriveScene 3D - NSW app, published by DriveScene ("we").

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your lesson answers and completion history are stored locally on your device and are never sent to us.

In-app purchase

The optional one-time purchase is processed by Apple. The app receives and verifies your purchase status from Apple to unlock lessons. We do not receive your payment card details or Apple Account credentials. “Restore purchases” uses your Apple Account.

External links

Lessons link to NSW Government websites for source material. Those websites receive normal connection information and have their own privacy policies.

Backups and deletion

Local app data may be included in device backups depending on your Apple settings. Deleting the app removes its local learning history from the device. A non-consumable purchase remains associated with your Apple Account and can be restored.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If our practices change, for example if we add analytics, we will update this page and the effective date above.
