Frequently asked questions

Which lessons are free?

Ten lessons covering commonly misunderstood NSW road rules are free. A single one-time purchase unlocks all remaining lessons. There is no subscription and no account.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the purchase screen (tap Unlock all lessons) and choose Restore purchases. Restoring uses the Apple Account that made the original purchase, so sign in with the same account on the device.

The price or purchase button does not appear.

The app loads the price from the App Store, so it needs an internet connection the first time. Check your connection, then tap Try again. Free lessons stay usable in the meantime.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my progress saved?

Your answers and completed lessons are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them, unless they are restored from a device backup.

Is this the official driver knowledge test?

No. DriveScene 3D - NSW is an independent educational app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NSW Government or Transport for NSW, and it does not reproduce official test questions. Each lesson links to the NSW Government guidance it is based on. Animations are simplified illustrations and do not show real safe speeds or gaps. Always check current official rules.

I found an error in a lesson.

Please email us with the lesson number and what you think is wrong, ideally with a link to the official source.
